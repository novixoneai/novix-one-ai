## ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

### ❌ Mistake 1: Waiting for "Perfect" Blog Posts
- Your blog posts are good. Publish them.
- Perfect is the enemy of done.

### ❌ Mistake 2: Expecting Instant Rankings
- New sites take 2-6 weeks to see movement
- Blog posts take 4-8 weeks to rank
- Backlinks take 2-6 weeks to have impact
- This is normal. Trust the process.

### ❌ Mistake 3: Skipping Backlinks
- Blog posts alone won't get you page 1 fast enough
- Backlinks are the accelerator
- Even 3-5 quality backlinks = massive impact

### ❌ Mistake 4: Writing Too Many Blog Posts
- 3 good posts > 10 mediocre posts
- Focus on depth and quality first
- Add more posts after month 2
