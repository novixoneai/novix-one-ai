## ONGOING: Monthly Monitoring

### Metrics to Track
1. **Google Search Console**
   - Impressions (how often you appear in search results)
   - Clicks (how often people click through)
   - Average position (ranking position)
   - Click-through rate (CTR)

2. **Keyword Rankings**
   - Track your 5 main keywords in a spreadsheet
   - Check weekly or bi-weekly using:
     - Google Search Console
     - SEMrush (free tier)
     - Ahrefs (free tier)

3. **Traffic**
   - Monitor Google Analytics for organic traffic growth
   - Expected: 10-50 visits/month by month 3

4. **Backlinks**
   - Monitor new backlinks using Ahrefs or SEMrush
   - Disavow spammy links if needed
