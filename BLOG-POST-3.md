## Why Human Receptionists Aren't the Answer

### The Hidden Costs of Full-Time Receptionists

**Direct costs:**
- Salary: $35,000-50,000/year
- Benefits (health, taxes): $8,000-15,000/year
- Software (phone system, CRM): $2,000-5,000/year
- **Total: $45,000-70,000/year**

**Indirect costs:**
- Training and onboarding: 2-4 weeks
- Time away from desk for breaks: 1-2 hours/day
- Turnover (hiring replacement): $5,000-10,000 per turnover
- Quality issues (bad customer experience): lost leads

**Real total cost: $50,000-100,000+/year**

### The Problems

**Problem 1: They Take Breaks**
- Even the best receptionist needs lunch, bathroom breaks, days off
- Every break = missed calls

**Problem 2: They Can't Handle Volume**
- 1 receptionist = 20-30 calls max per day
- Busy personal injury firm? 50-100 calls per day
- Overflow calls = voicemail

**Problem 3: Quality Varies**
- Some receptionists are great at client interaction
- Others are rude or dismissive
- First impression matters for client retention
- Bad interaction = negative review + lost referrals

**Problem 4: Limited Information**
- Receptionist may forget details
- Messages get lost or miscommunicated
- Lead qualification is inconsistent
- You lose valuable context about the caller's needs

**Problem 5: No After-Hours Coverage**
- Emergencies don't care what time it is
- Personal injury happens at 2am
- DUI arrests happen on weekends
- You're completely dark after 5pm

**Problem 6: Turnover Costs**
- Average receptionist tenure: 2-3 years
- Replacing them costs $5,000-10,000
- Every replacement = knowledge loss + setup time
- Happens every few years
