## Tactic 3: Directory & Citation Listings

### Why This Matters
Local + industry directories signal authority to Google for your Miami-focused keywords.

### Where to List (30-60 mins total):

**Free Listings:**
1. Google Business Profile (if not already done)
   - Add all service categories
   - Post regular updates
   - Upload photos
   - URL: novixone.co

2. LinkedIn Company Page
   - Complete profile
   - Add company website
   - Link to blog posts

3. B2B Directories (Free Tier):
   - G2 (free reviews + company profile)
   - Capterra (free company profile)
   - Clutch (law firms specifically)

4. Local Directories:
   - Miami Chamber of Commerce
   - Better Business Bureau (BBB)
   - Yelp for Business

**Paid Listings (Optional):**
- Upland (B2B platform) — $0-500/year
- Industry-specific directories

### Expected Results
- 5-10 listings = local authority signals
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone) = ranking boost
- Cost: Free to $500
- Authority boost: +2-5 DA points

## Tracking Your Backlinks

### Tools (Free Tier):
1. **Google Search Console**
   - Go to Links → External Links
   - See all new backlinks

2. **Ahrefs Free Tier**
   - Enter domain
   - See backlinks report

3. **SEMrush Free Tier**
   - Backlink Analytics
   - Competitor backlinks

### What to Monitor
- Number of backlinks (target: 3-5 in week 2-3)
- Authority of linking site (prefer DA 30+)
- Anchor text (should include your keywords)
- Referring domain diversity (don't all come from same site)
